The Signature Experience
Under 4 Players? You get a pro

Short a player? You'll tee off with a retired NFL player or pro athlete

This isn't just a round of golf — it's a chance to trade stories, get tips, and share the course with the athletes who make this tournament unlike any other charity scramble in Georgia. Register solo, as a duo, or as a team of 3, and we'll fill out your foursome with a pro.

Why Play

A day of golf that changes lives

All proceeds benefit the Ridgeview Alumni Charitable Corporation, supporting individuals seeking treatment for substance use and mental health challenges.

🏊

Play With Pros

Join former NFL players and professional athletes on the course. Any team smaller than a foursome gets paired with a retired NFL player or pro athlete.

Friendly Competition

A scramble format built for fun, with prizes for top teams, closest to pin, longest drive, and a hole-in-one contest.

Support Recovery

Every dollar raised helps provide treatment and housing assistance for people rebuilding their lives.
